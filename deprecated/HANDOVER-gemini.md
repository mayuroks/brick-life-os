# HANDOVER.md: Life OS & Autonomous Agentic Framework

> **Objective:** Eliminate mental friction, externalize environmental and logistical preparation, automate background research with a 1-week horizon, and transform raw brain dumps into an execution-ready daily schedule linked directly to your 14-inch MacBook Pro calendar and chat interface.

---

## 1. System Architecture & Core Objectives

### The Buckets (Database Layer)

Your core projects are mapped across 9 master vectors in Jira (or local markdown files if migrating):

1. **Career**
2. **Family**
3. **House Work**
4. **Finance**
5. **Relation / Network**
6. **Health / Diet**
7. **Life OS System (Upgrade/Maintenance)**
8. **Docs**
9. **Random Ideas**

### The Core Objectives

* **Zero-Friction Ingestion:** Drop raw text or voice notes via Telegram/Discord at any time without worrying about organization.
* **Environmental & Logistical Control:** Force the system to prep physical items (gym duffel checklist) and human dependencies (maid dietary specs/recipes) alongside digital tasks.
* **Proactive Research Horizon:** Complex items are researched 1 week in advance by the AI agent so execution starts with zero lag.
* **Execution Simplicity:** Wake up, check the calendar/chat feed, and execute without administrative overhead.

---

## 2. Operating Ceremonies & Workflows

### A. The Nightly Prep (10:15 PM — 15-Minute Protocol)

* **Action:** You send a quick voice note or text brain-dump to the Telegram/Discord bot containing daily thoughts, updates, and raw tasks.
* **Agent Responsibilities:**
1. Parse the dump and populate/update Jira items across the 9 buckets (`Backlog` $\rightarrow$ `Ready`).
2. Map tasks for the next day into specific calendar blocks.
3. Generate the **Environmental & Logistical Checklist** (e.g., gym gear, dietary specs for the household help).
4. Inject the core personal ambition ("the dream") into the daily brief to anchor execution motivation.



### B. The Morning Execution (Wake Up)

* **Action:** Open your calendar or eck your Discord/Telegram channel.
* **What You See:**
* The day's time blocks and task agenda.
* Routine checklists (gym routine, pre-workout, nutrition).
* Environment checklist (duffel packed, maid instructions ready).


* **Execution:** Plug into the plan and execute. No manual scheduling required.

### C. The Weekly Retro & 1-Week Horizon Scan (Sunday)

* **Action:** Triggered weekly by you or run automatically via cron.
* **Agent Responsibilities:**
1. **Telemetry Review:** Calculate velocity, completed vs. delayed items across your 8–9 projects, and bottleneck analysis.
2. **Proactive Horizon Scan:** Look 7 days ahead into upcoming tasks, spin up background research sessions, and draft specs so they land in your `Ready` queue before the week begins.



---

## 3. Feedback Loop & Agent Directives

If any adjustments, corrections, or mid-week pivots are needed, do not fiddle with complex software interfaces.

* **The Channel:** Open your dedicated **Discord** or **Telegram** channel.
* **The Command: Message the agent directly (e.g., *"Shift the finance review to Thursday afternoon, update the maid's recipe for tomorrow to low-carb, and push the career deep-dive to next week."*).
* **The Result:** The agent ingests the instruction, updates Jira, restructures the calendar, and confirms execution back to your chat.
